## Funded direction

A transaction hash is public chain evidence, not cryptographic proof that the issue author controls the sending wallet. A hash cannot be reused. A donation may direct a request but does not purchase support, ownership, investment returns, a delivery deadline, priority, acceptance, or unsafe, illegal, private, proprietary, or out-of-scope work.

Never post a private key, seed phrase, wallet signature, exchange credential, or other secret.
